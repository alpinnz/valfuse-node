# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The valfuse-node community is committed to a respectful, safe, and inclusive
environment for everyone who participates, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for the effect of your words and actions.
- Credit the sources of code, ideas, and other contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging harm against a person or group.
- Sharing another person's private information without their consent.
- Impersonation, deliberate disruption, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Other conduct that would make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code
reviews, and project communications. It also applies when someone represents
valfuse-node in public.

## Reporting

Report a suspected violation privately using the contact information on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/alpinnz). Do not post reports or
private details in public issues or pull requests. Include relevant links and
context, and avoid sharing unrelated personal information.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and keep them confidential as far as
possible. If a report concerns the maintainer or the profile contact is
unavailable, use GitHub's reporting tools for the relevant content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit content and may issue a warning, restrict
participation temporarily, or ban a participant. The response will depend on the
severity and context of the behavior; severe cases may skip earlier steps.
